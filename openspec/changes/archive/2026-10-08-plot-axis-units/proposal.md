## Why

Prediction plots titled their axes with the quantity only ("Wet weight",
"Stipe density"), so a reader could not tell the units without the code.

## What Changes

- Axis titles give the unit in parentheses for every quantity that has one,
  for example "Wet weight (kg)", "Stipe density (stipes/m²)", "Wet biomass
  (kg/m²)", and "Total carbon biomass (kg C)". Counts, proportions, ratios,
  and grouping factors keep a plain title.
- The wet/dry and carbon responses gain descriptive titles.

## Non-goals

- No change to which variables are plotted or how.
